# Brand and landing brief

## Brand idea

A calm control surface for work that used to require a team. The brand should feel capable, precise and alive without looking like a sci-fi command center. Show outcomes, evidence and agency. Avoid infinite-agent diagrams, generic robot imagery and claims of complete autonomy.

## Suggested language

Working concepts: “Install work into your company.” “Give your business a mission.” “From context to useful work.” “Discover what your company can delegate.” These are directions, not final copy or a selected name.

## Visual direction

Warm neutral base, black typography, one electric accent and semantic colors reserved for state. Use crisp object cards, evidence links, timelines and progressive disclosure. The visual metaphor is an operational workspace with living capabilities, not a dashboard full of metrics.

Typography should optimize long-form readability and dense but calm work views. Use diagrams to explain relationships, not as decorative complexity. Brand and UI color tokens must distinguish suggestion, test, active, waiting and error without depending on color alone.

## Landing structure

1. Hero: “Tell us how your company works. Start with one piece of work.” Interactive input for website or explanation.
2. Immediate result: sample profile and recommended capabilities.
3. Three paths: recommendations, browse marketplace, describe your own need.
4. Demonstration: source → configured mission → evaluated result → supervised activation.
5. Capability gallery: several sectors and functions with real package statuses.
6. Trust: sources, approvals, permissions, costs, export and audit.
7. Expert layer: BYOK, custom instructions, tools and evaluation datasets.
8. CTA: “See what your company can delegate.”

## Claims discipline

Use measured examples with sample size, conditions and date. Do not claim “autonomous company”, revenue or “live in five minutes” unless the displayed path supports it. Explain that external effects require user permission. Show a product walkthrough rather than a static AI diagram.

## Creative system

Produce: company-profile reveal; capability card carousel; before/after workload; evaluation lab recording; source-to-result timeline; multi-capability composition map; expert harness view; cost/quality comparison; human approval moment. Each creative must show one concrete input and output.
