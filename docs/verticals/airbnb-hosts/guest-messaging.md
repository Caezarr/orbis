# Install guest messaging into your company.

**H1:** Install guest messaging into your company.

**H2:** You already paste guest threads into ChatGPT. Orbis turns that into a bounded mission on *your* listings: a reply draft with sources and unknowns, then you decide.

**CTA:** Test a mission on my company → `/audit?vertical=airbnb-hosts&job=guest-messaging`

**Micro:** Website or 4 questions. First deliverable to review. Nothing acts outside without you.

**ICP:** For Airbnb hosts drowning in Wi-Fi and check-in threads. Not hotel contact-center software.

### Motion
1. Late check-in question → Reply prepared · Sources: house manual
2. Noise complaint → Reply prepared · Unknowns flagged if stay notes missing
3. Extra guest ask → Reply prepared · Policy line from your listing

### FAQ
**Will Orbis message the guest?** Not by default.
**House rules?** From audit/sources; missing = unknowns.
